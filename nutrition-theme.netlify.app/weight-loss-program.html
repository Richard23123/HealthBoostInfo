Recommended plan

Nutrition plan for steady, sustainable progress.

This is the destination used when the questionnaire identifies Nutrition as the main focus. It is structured as a practical plan, not a generic sales page.

Retake questionnaireReview nutrition area
Plan structure

Start with habits you can repeat

Use the result from the questionnaire to choose a realistic entry point. The plan can later be expanded with real professional guidance.

01

Organize meals

Pick three core meals you can prepare without stress. Keep ingredients simple and familiar.

02

Support energy

Use hydration, protein and fiber to reduce energy drops during the day.

03

Review progress

Check what worked each week and adjust one habit at a time instead of changing everything.

What to improve first

Choose one nutrition lever

  • Meal timing if you skip meals often.
  • Hydration if energy drops before afternoon.
  • Protein and fiber if hunger feels difficult to manage.
  • Grocery planning if decisions feel rushed.
Next step

Connect the plan

If your result also showed stress or low activity, use the secondary recommendations instead of treating nutrition as isolated.
